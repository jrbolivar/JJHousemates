StepMates privacy

StepMates is a small app for family and friends to compare daily step counts.

Health data

  • With your permission, StepMates reads only your daily step count from Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android). It reads nothing else and never writes to them.
  • Steps are read only while the app is open.
  • Your daily totals for the last few weeks are saved to your StepMates account so you see your history on any device.

Who sees what

  • Your history is private to you.
  • Your name and daily totals are shown only to members of groups you join. Leave a group and your steps are removed from it.
  • Nothing is sold, used for ads or shared with anyone else.

Where it's stored

Accounts and step totals are stored in Google Firebase. To delete your account and data, contact the person who invited you to StepMates.
